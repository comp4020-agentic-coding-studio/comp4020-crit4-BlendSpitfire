# Crit 4 reflection

## What was the breakthrough that moved the work forward?

As mentioned in the process notes, the biggest breakthrough was using a
small interactive helper tool to align on requirements directly, instead of
negotiating them through language.

## What did this work change about who I want to be as a software developer?

The task itself wasn't hard, so I don't think it changed much on its own.
But lately I've been noticing that AI is often smarter and more trustworthy
than I expect in some respects, while at the same time it still has clear
gaps in how it handles many features and tasks. I think what's really
happening is that I'm becoming more familiar with what Claude is and isn't
good at — and that's quietly pushing me to refine my own workflow around it.
